Donegeon can now remind you of things, even with the app closed.

* Make your own reminders in **Settings › Notifications**: what's in Today, what's still open, tomorrow, next week, a recap of your week, the deadlines coming up, or your own words.
* Pick the times, every day or only some days.
* A task left undone goes to the bell the next day, so nothing slips through.
